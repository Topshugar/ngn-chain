01 — Abstract

NGN Chain is a public, EVM-compatible blockchain designed for global, borderless value transfer.

Anyone, anywhere can create a non-custodial wallet in seconds, receive NGN from the faucet, mine NGN by securing the network, send NGN to anyone in seconds, and build decentralized applications using standard Ethereum tools.

NGN Chain has a fixed maximum supply of 10,000,000,000 NGN. New NGN is created only through mining. There is no pre-mine sale, no fixed price, and no peg. The price of NGN is free-floating and decided entirely by the market.

One line summary: NGN Chain = Global money rails, owned by its community, not by any bank or government.

02 — Vision & Mission

Vision

A world where anyone can send and receive value as easily as sending a message — instant, cheap, and without permission.

Mission

To provide a simple, open, and secure blockchain that the whole world can use — from first-time users in Lagos to developers in London, traders in Dubai, and builders in New York.

NGN Chain is not just for Nigeria. The name NGN reflects its origin. The mission is global.

03 — The Problem

  • Slow & expensive transfers: International transfers still take days and cost 5-10% in fees.
  • Excluded people: Over 1.4 billion people have no bank account, but most have a phone.
  • Too complex: Most blockchains require MetaMask, gas knowledge, and technical setup. Ordinary people get left out.
  • Centralized control: Many “crypto” projects are controlled by a small team that can freeze, mint, or change rules.

04 — The Solution: NGN Chain

NGN Chain solves this with 4 principles:

  • 1. Simple: Built-in NGN Wallet. No MetaMask needed. Create wallet in 10 seconds.
  • 2. Open: Anyone can mine, transact, and build. No approval, no KYC to use the chain.
  • 3. EVM-Compatible: Developers can use Solidity, Hardhat, Remix, and standard Ethereum tooling.
  • 4. Transparent: Fixed supply, public explorer, public rules. No hidden minting.

05 — How NGN Chain Works

Network

NGN Chain is an EVM-compatible Proof-of-Work (PoW) blockchain. Chain ID is 777. Blocks are produced by miners who solve cryptographic puzzles. Each valid block adds transactions to the chain and rewards the miner.

Transactions

Users sign transactions with their private key in NGN Wallet. Transactions are broadcast to the network, included in a block by a miner, and confirmed by the network. Explorer shows all blocks and transactions publicly.

Smart Contracts

Because NGN Chain is EVM-compatible, developers can deploy standard Solidity smart contracts for tokens, DeFi, NFTs, payments, and more via NGN Studio.

Supply Logic

Max supply is capped at 10 Billion NGN. New NGN enters circulation only via block rewards (50 NGN per block). Once max supply is reached, no new NGN can be created.

Price — Free Float

NGN has no fixed price, no peg to Naira, Dollar, or any fiat, and no price guarantee. Price is determined solely by supply and demand on open markets where NGN is traded. NGN Chain does not control, set, or guarantee price.

Distribution: NGN is distributed via mining and faucet — not via an ICO or private sale. This keeps distribution community-driven and transparent.

07 — Mining & Faucet

Mining

Anyone can mine NGN using NGN Miner App. Miners compete to solve the block puzzle. The first miner to solve it earns 50 NGN and the block is added to the chain. Mining secures the network and distributes NGN fairly.

Faucet

The Faucet gives new users 100 NGN per 24 hours per address to try the network, test sending, and start trading. The Faucet is for onboarding and testing — it is not a source of income and is rate-limited to prevent abuse.

08 — Wallet & Security

NGN Wallet is non-custodial. You control your keys. NGN Chain never holds, sees, or can recover your funds.

  • Your wallet is created from a 12-word seed phrase (BIP-39 standard).
  • Your private key never leaves your device.
  • If you lose your 12 words, your wallet cannot be recovered by anyone — including NGN Chain.
  • Never share your seed phrase. NGN Chain team will NEVER ask for it.
  • Always verify you are on ngn-chain.onrender.com before entering your seed phrase.
⚠️ Security Warning: Anyone who asks for your seed phrase is trying to steal your funds. No support agent, no admin, no website will ever need it. Keep it on paper, offline, in a safe place.
