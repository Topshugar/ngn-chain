🌍 What is NGN Chain?

NGN Chain is a public, permissionless blockchain. It is not just for Nigeria — NGN is a global asset for anyone who wants fast, low-cost, borderless value transfer.

  • Global from day one: Anyone in any country can join, mine, and transact.
  • EVM-compatible: Works with standard Ethereum tools, wallets, and smart contracts.
  • Community-mined: New NGN is created only by miners securing the network. No pre-mine sale.
  • Non-custodial: You hold your own keys. NGN Chain never holds or controls your funds.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is NGN only for Nigerians?

No. NGN Chain is global. The name NGN comes from its origin, but anyone in the world can use, mine, and build on it.

What is the total supply?

Max supply is 10,000,000,000 NGN (10 Billion). New NGN is only created via mining rewards of 50 NGN per block until max supply is reached.

What is the price of NGN?

NGN is free-floating. There is no fixed price, no peg, and no guarantee. The market decides based on supply and demand.

Do I need MetaMask?

No. NGN Wallet is built-in. You can create a wallet in 10 seconds with a 12-word backup. MetaMask is optional for advanced users.

What if I lose my 12 words?

Your wallet cannot be recovered by anyone, including NGN Chain. Keep your seed phrase on paper in a safe place. Never share it with anyone.

Is NGN Chain audited?

NGN Chain is currently in public test / early mainnet phase. Use with amounts you can afford to lose. A formal security audit is part of Phase 2.
